# Practices

- The premise is the problem in a person's language. The hash is not the name of the hypothesis.
- Each cause stays on the hypothesis that assumes it. Several causes do not become one campaign sentence.
- `context` is the first read of the session. `frontier` answers what still competes. `why` answers one hypothesis.
- Record a complete trial for every metric in the schema. The frontier ignores an incomplete trial.
- Leave the worse trial in the history. It documents the attempt.
- Reject only with a motive written by the one who decided. Dominance is not death.
- Use `.acvcignore` in the seeded folder for what is not the hypothesis. Do not rely on Git's `.gitignore`.
- The store follows `--repo`. The markers stay in the seeded folder.
- Show the premise, the status, and the metric. Show the hash only when the agent must pass the identifier to another command.
- `recall` with one word from the premise, including an accent. An empty search, or one made only of punctuation, is an error.
- `spend` records what the session actually consumed. It is not a budget, not a metric, and it does not block `record`.
- The page, TUI, VS Code, and Obsidian already speak in premises. Do not reopen SQLite to "improve" the viewer.
